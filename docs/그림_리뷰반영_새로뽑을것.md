## 1. 풀려난 여포 — `lvbu_free.webp`
- **가장 좋은 방법:** 지금 여포 초상(`assets/portrait/lvbu.webp`)을 함께 올리고 아래 문장을 붙인다. 얼굴과 갑옷이 그대로 이어져야 한다.
- 크기와 모양: 지금 초상과 같은 세로 그림, 무릎 위까지, 배경은 투명.

```
Same character as the reference image, same face, same armor and pheasant-feather headdress.
Remove all ropes and bindings from his chest and arms. He stands free, holding a long halberd (fangtian ji) upright in his right hand.
Proud but restrained expression. Painted semi-realistic style matching the reference, warm side light.
Knee-up portrait, transparent background, no text.
```

## 3. 추를 달기 전의 작업장 — `ch8_workshop_old.webp`
- 핵심: **매달린 돌 상자나 추가 하나도 없어야 한다.** 줄을 여러 가닥 묶어 사람들이 당겨서 던지는 옛 발석거만 있어야 한다.
- 가로 1672×940, 지금 7장 그림들과 같은 색감.

```
Late Eastern Han military workshop at Guandu, daytime, overcast sky, muddy ground.
Several traction trebuchets under construction: a long wooden throwing arm on an A-frame, with many ropes hanging from the short end for crews of men to pull by hand.
No counterweight, no hanging box of stones, no hanging weight of any kind.
Carpenters marking timber with ink lines, piles of logs, stones for ammunition, Cao army banners with the character 曹 on dark blue cloth.
Painted cinematic style, wide landscape 16:9, no text other than banners.
```
